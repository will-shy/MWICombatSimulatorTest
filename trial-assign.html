MWI Trial Assignment

Split the guild between two trial bosses · For IC guild Arsenal internal use only. Group Battle Skill Lab

Upload the guild's combat-trial export, pick the week's two bosses and how many players each team takes, and the page finds the split with the highest trial score (200 per tier cleared, plus 100 × progress into the next, summed over both bosses). Every member fights on the Build Guide's bar for their build, with the guide's triggers. It measures each member alone in a fixed reference raid on both bosses, calibrates each boss's ladder, plans the split from those numbers, then verifies the best few splits on the full ladder, all on the same wasm kernel as Group Battle. Each team gets the role counts and debuff coverage set below. Load the guild site's current assignment to see how it does and compare it with the best split.
