Reading the tables

Team tiers, and the class's own DPS

most team tiers tied with the top (one-sided Mann-Whitney p > 0.05) the build in the plan

The big number is team tiers: tiers cleared plus the fraction of the next tier's boss HP removed, averaged over 10 runs. So 7.53 means T7 cleared and the T8 boss 53% down. The small number is the changed players' average DPS over the whole ladder. Hover a cell for the spread, the p-value and raid DPS.

Pick rule: the build with the most team tiers. Among builds tied with it, the one with the higher class DPS. Badger, Chameleon and Swarm use up the full 3600 s budget, so tiers there measure damage output and separate cleanly. Jellyfish and Hedgehog end when the raid wipes, so most builds come out tied there and the pick falls to class DPS.

Debuffs

The raid hits one enemy at a time

Every player's single-target skills go to the first living enemy (CombatUtilities.getTarget). On Swarm that's Beetle, then Dragonfly, Wasp and Firefly in turn. So the right measure of a single-target debuff is its uptime on the current target. The table shows that for the baseline raid: Fire, Water and Nature players as imported, supports on Smoke Burst.

  • Smoke Burst is already near its ceiling. With all 7 Nature Supports on it, plus the DPS who carry it, the current target on Swarm stays smoked 89% of the time. That's despite about half the casts missing, and the debuff only applies on a hit. Averaged over all four Swarm enemies it is only 27%, because the three waiting enemies are rarely hit.
  • Frost Surge is the debuff with room left. It hits every enemy but holds only about half the time, so a build that lands it more often raises it for the whole raid.

  • Firestorm gives the two Water players the most damage, about 27 DPS more than Smoke Burst. But the other 56 players do about 43 DPS less, and the team ends 0.03 tiers behind Smoke Burst and 0.07 behind Precision + Ice Spear.
  • The builds that help everyone else add a debuff. Precision makes the Water players' Frost Surge land more often, raising its uptime on the target from about 47% to 55%, and it has the most team tiers. Smoke Burst adds a little Smoke uptime and is tied with it. Firestorm, Ice Spear and Life Drain add no debuff. Enemy accuracy, damage taken and deaths don't change between builds, so the gap is all offense.
  • The plan takes Smoke Burst over Precision because the two are tied on tiers and Smoke Burst gives the Water players slightly more DPS. Either is fine.
Caveats

What this doesn't settle

  • Small classes, small effects. Water is 2 players, and Fire and Nature DPS are 1 each. Their builds move team tiers by a few hundredths on the budget-limited bosses, and not detectably on Jellyfish and Hedgehog at 10 runs. Picks there come down to the class's own DPS.
  • Level 40 everywhere. The real bars run skills at levels 45–68, so these builds are compared with each other, not with what players field today.
  • One class at a time. Fire, Water and Nature were each varied with the other two left as imported. They share Smoke Burst and Frost Surge, and neither stacks across casters, so a joint run could shift the picks.
  • Different rosters. Cursed Bow and Ranged ran on 56 players with the supports as imported; Fire, Water and Nature on 58 with the supports standardised. Compare tiers within a table, not across sections.
  • Guild buildings are the harness defaults. The export doesn't carry them.
