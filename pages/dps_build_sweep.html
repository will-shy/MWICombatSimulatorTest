  • Who is changed. Within a class, only players running Insanity get the build. Players carrying a raid aura keep their bar, because swapping the aura out would change the whole raid. That leaves CastorTroyRanged, AzureCamilla, WillZ, dimibay, Tinbad, Jinbad (Ranged) and OTTOWAAO (Cursed Bow) as imported.   • Supports are fixed. Anyone bringing Mana Spring is a Mana Support and keeps their bar. Anyone bringing Rejuvenate without Mana Spring is a Nature Support, whatever their weapon, and runs their slot-1 ability + Rejuvenate + Smoke Burst + Toxic Pollen + Entangle in every Fire, Water and Nature run. Six of the seven already did; maage swaps Precision for Smoke Burst (Lv 40). The Cursed Bow and Ranged runs came before this and use the supports as imported.
  • Fire. Nobody in the Hedgehog roster uses a fire weapon, so Dedende (blazing trident, CL132, Insanity) was taken from the 28 Sep Badger export and added as a 57th player. The Fire runs use that 57-player raid. Monsters scale with party size, so the extra player raises the raid DPS baseline by 1.5–5% per boss. Compare Fire's numbers with each other, not with the other sections'.
  • Fixed rows were chosen and held in place for the Water and Nature runs. Proposed rows are this page's recommendation and are not confirmed yet.
Reading the tables

How each cell is scored

best on this boss statistically tied with the best (one-sided Mann-Whitney p > 0.05) the build in the plan

Hover a cell for the mean, spread and progress behind it. Progress is tiers cleared plus the fraction of the next tier's boss HP removed, so 7.35 means T7 cleared and the T8 boss 35% down. Badger, Chameleon and Swarm use up the full 3600 s budget on every run, so progress there measures damage output and the spread is tight. Jellyfish and Hedgehog end when the raid wipes, so their numbers are noisier.

Caveats

What this doesn't settle

  • Small classes, small effects. Water is 2 players, and Fire and Nature DPS are 1 each, out of a 56–57-player raid. Their builds move raid DPS by 3% at most, often inside the run-to-run spread. The close calls need about 40 runs to separate.
  • Level 40 everywhere. The real bars run skills at levels 45–68, so these builds are compared with each other, not with what players field today.
  • One class at a time. Fire, Water and Nature were each varied with the other two left as imported. All three can bring Smoke Burst, and Water and Nature can both bring Frost Surge. Neither debuff stacks across casters, so a joint run could shift the picks.
  • Guild buildings are the harness defaults. The export doesn't carry them.
