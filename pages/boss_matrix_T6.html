The Arsenal build guide's Boss Matrix scores each build against each boss from the combat formulas — hit chance times mitigation. This is the same eight builds run through the group-battle simulator instead: a real 50-player raid, the guide's own recommended loadout for each boss, fought to the kill at tier 6.

The score

One point is one percent of the boss group's health per minute, per player

Each build's score is the share of the enemy group's total HP that one player of that build removes each minute. Because the whole raid fights one kill, the eight scores plus the three support squads' add up to the real thing: score × players × kill time = 100% of the boss group. Badger and Swarm are scored against the combined health of every body in the group, not one member.

The guide's own numbers appear under every cell. They are a different quantity — a formula index, not a rate — so read the comparison as ordering and spread, not as a difference of two measurements of the same thing. ◂ marks each build's best boss in the sim.

Disagreements

The four worth arguing about

These are the cells where the sim's ordering would change a real assignment decision, not merely break one of the guide's ties.

Hedgehog

Fire: the guide's pick, fourth in the sim

Fire is mana-starved, not outclassed. 56 casts per player are blocked for want of mana over a 8.9-minute fight. Give it mana and it scores 0.327 and takes first place, which is the guide's call.

Swarm

Smash: mid-pack in the guide, first in the sim

The guide rates the three melee styles within 0.004 of each other on Swarm. Fracturing Impact hits all four bodies while Maim and Puncture hit one, so Smash outscores Slash by 45% and Stab by 42%.

Jellyfish

Stab: the guide's floor, the sim's third place

The guide rates all three melee styles at 0.118, its lowest value on this boss. Puncture's −20% armor and the Furious Spear put Stab at 0.288 in the sim — third, behind only the two ranged builds, and ahead of all three casters.

Swarm

Bow: level with Crossbow in the guide, 11% behind it in the sim

The guide's Swarm tweak drops Pestilent Shot for Penetrating Shot plus Rain of Arrows. Rain of Arrows is 20% of Bow's damage, but Crossbow keeps Pestilent Shot in its own Swarm tweak and scores 0.192 against Bow's 0.173 — the −10% armor and −10% all-resistance debuff is worth more than the second area ability.

The mana finding

Two Mana Spring supports are not enough for the magic builds

Fire, Water and Nature run 0-cooldown fillers, and with two Mana Spring supports in a fifty-player group they spend a large part of the fight unable to pay for a cast. Re-running the whole matrix with mana switched off isolates the cost: the physical builds gain a few percent, the magic builds gain 13–42%. That gap is the single largest correction in this study, and it is a roster problem rather than a build problem.

Method

How these numbers were produced

Roster
50 players. Support: WarkTank 2 · Nature Support 5 · Mana Spring Support 2. DPS: Smash 6 · Slash 5 · Stab 5 · Bow 5 · Crossbow 5 · Fire 5 · Water 5 · Nature DPS 5. 41 DPS over 8 builds leaves one spare; it went to Smash, whose Fracturing Impact is the raid's only +damage-taken debuff and benefits everyone. Scores are per player, so the uneven squad does not flatter its own number.
Loadouts
The guide's recommended kit for that boss, including every listed tweak — Steady Shot for Bow and Crossbow on Chameleon and Jellyfish, Precision instead of Elemental Affinity for the casters on Chameleon, and the Swarm variants.
Gear and levels
The T95 sets under data/testPlayers. All seven skills forced to 125, every house room at level 4 (+4 to all combat skills, +2% attack speed, +0.02 cast speed, +0.12pp regen). Ability levels: auras 25, the three 0-cooldown spells (Water Strike, Entangle, Fireball) 60, everything else 40.
Assumptions
Players take no damage, so nobody dies and the measurement is pure output. Every raid debuff is present and held up: Fracturing Impact, Maim, Puncture, Pestilent Shot, Smoke Burst, Frost Surge, Ice Spear and Toxic Pollen all have a live source. Debuffs are keyed by type in the engine, so extra casters buy uptime, never a bigger number.
Monsters
Rebuilt from game data at room level 150 with 50-player scaling: +50% max HP, +100% attack and cast speed, +100 ability haste. Enrage runs as normal (+10% monster damage and accuracy per 10 minutes), which affects incoming damage only.
Runs
Three seeded runs per boss, every one a victory in 8.2–10.6 minutes. Damage is summed over summed durations, so the score is a true rate. Seed spread on kill time was under 7%.
Reproducing a cell
Every number comes from src/combatsimulator/skillLab.js — the same core the Skill Lab page runs — driven over the 40 cells by src/skillLabMatrix.js. Enter the same squads and kits in skill-lab.html at level 150 and the page reproduces the cell.
Raw output
pages/data/boss_matrix_T6.json and …_infinite_mana.json carry per-ability damage, hit rates, cast counts and blocked casts for every squad.
