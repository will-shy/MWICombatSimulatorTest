What limits these numbers

Score is within-build; Norm is cross-build

A 100 in Fire (3 players) and a 100 in Smash (31 players) are not the same achievement. For cross-class balancing use Norm — on that scale the top Smash is 476K against the top Crossbow’s 240K.

Nature DPS’s cross-class position rests on an assumption

It appears only on Hedgehog, so its within-class ranking is solid but its level relative to other builds is not. At the assumed 0.59 factor WillT normalises to 336K, above every crossbow; at a crossbow-like 0.85 he would be 233K, roughly tied with RedGreenBlue. Pinning this down needs nature players on Hedgehog. Fire and Flex DPS have the same problem.

Water’s 0.32 is the shakiest number here

It rests on 3 Hedgehog players, two of whom — AsikkamykX at 438K and xaivaicc at 387K — are the guild’s top two damage dealers overall. That inflates the two Hedgehog water players to about 310K normalised. Treat those scores as provisional.

Support scores are close to meaningless

Nature Support, Mana Spring Support and Wark are scored on damage as requested, but healing is their real output — ccp003 scores 86 on damage while posting the guild’s highest heal at 1,014K.

The sheets record total damage, not damage per second

Damage taken clusters tightly within each sheet — 187–221K on Hedgehog, 260–335K on Hedgehog — so encounter length looks common and totals are proportional to DPS. But they are totals.
