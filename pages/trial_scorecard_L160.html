What the pattern rules out

Because ranged reconciles, no global factor can explain the rest. Party size, the HP denominator, aura level and time-window choice would all move the three classes together. The melee and magic gaps have to come from per-build inputs. Two probes on this side: giving the smasher full maelstrom legs instead of the preset's anchorbound legs is worth +4%, and switching magic to the AoE kit on Badger is −14%, not a gain — so the specified single-target kit is already the better one there.

Four questions that would close the gap

1. Do your melee and magic builds carry house rooms or achievements? The presets here have neither, and maxed combat rooms plus the Elite tier are worth roughly the 16–26% seen on melee. 2. What enhancement levels and jewellery are on the nature build? 3. Was your Swarm ranged kit still running Rain of Arrows? 4. Are the Hedgehog and Swarm rows measured or carried forward? Hedgehog reads 0.136 for all three classes and Swarm reads 0.180 / 0.183 / 0.180 — that uniformity is unlike anything the engine produces, and 0.136 appears four times in the table.

Findings

What the kit changes cost and bought

On Swarm, the ranged squad needs its AoE back

Running Pestilent Shot + Penetrating Shot against four mobs puts ranged last at 1.17 %/min. Both single-target — the squad is the only one in that fight with no AoE, while melee gets 15% of its damage from Fracturing Impact and magic 34% from Nature's Veil.

Swarm · ranged 4th + 5th slotRanged %/minRaid kill time
Pestilent + Penetrating (current)1.1720.0 min
Pestilent + Rain of Arrows1.26 +8%19.6 min ◂ fastest
Penetrating + Rain of Arrows1.30 +11%20.1 min

Penetrating + Rain of Arrows scores the ranged squad highest, but Pestilent + Rain of Arrows kills the group fastest, because Pestilent Shot's −13.9% armor and resistance debuff is worth more to the other 28 damage dealers than to its own caster. Optimise for the clear, not the scoreboard.

The Chameleon and Jellyfish tweak is a clear win

Swapping Precision for Pestilent Shot alongside Steady Shot lifts ranged +20% in both fights — 0.128 → 0.153 %/min per player on Chameleon and 0.163 → 0.196 on Jellyfish — and pulls raid kill times down by two minutes each. Steady Shot lands at 84–86% accuracy, the highest hit rate of any ability in the study, and the armor strip carries the rest of the raid with it.

Auras are worth 14–18%, and they reorder nothing

The five auras sit at level 25 on the support placeholders — the only slots available, since every damage dealer keeps Insanity. Removing them entirely drops raid totals by 14–18% and adds two to four minutes per trial, but every class ordering above survives, so the index numbers hold either way.

Melee and ranged are auto-attackers; magic is not

Across all five trials, 85–93% of melee damage and 69–89% of ranged damage comes from auto-attacks — their abilities are mostly buffs and debuffs. Magic is the opposite: effectively all of its damage is cast, with Entangle filler alone accounting for 27–51% at 405–489 casts per fight. Attack speed is the physical squads' throughput lever; cast speed and ability haste are magic's.

Marginal levels

What one level is worth

Fitted over levels 115–135 in the L160 Swarm fight. Both stats enter combat ratings as (10 + level) × (1 + gear%), so one level is always ≈0.74% of the underlying rating — that ceiling is what the main attribute delivers.

BuildMain attributeAttackVerdict
Melee+0.70% / level (melee)+0.39% / levelmelee ≈ 1.8× attack
Ranged+0.74% / level (ranged)+0.78% / levelinterchangeable, attack marginally ahead
Magic+0.70% / level (magic)+0.60% / levelmagic ≈ 1.2× attack

Attack buys accuracy, which converts at 1.4 × (1 − hit rate) × 0.74% — so it is worth more the more you miss, and it keeps gaining value as tiers rise. Ranged is the exception that beats its own main attribute: ranged crit chance is 0.3 × hit chance, so accuracy buys crit rate too.

Method — headless run of the same code path as the Group Battle page (worker.js · case "start_battle"): real Player objects built from the T95 presets, zone buffs from /actions/combat/fly, group-battle regen buffs, monsters rebuilt from combatMonsterDetailMap at room level 160 and scaled for 52 players (+1% max HP, +2% attack speed, +2% cast speed, +2 ability haste per player). Damage read from simResult.attacks; five seeded runs per group; seed-to-seed spread on every figure above is under ±0.06 %/min.
Caveats — support placeholders deal exactly zero damage but still carry the auras. Monster crowd control still applies, since stuns and blinds are not damage. The presets carry no house rooms, achievements, shrines or labyrinth upgrades, so treat absolute %/min as a floor and the class ratios as the durable result.
