Normalised to the current matrix. The rerun fights L160 with a different roster from the one behind the current matrix, so its raw scores sit at a different level. Every new score is multiplied by one factor, k = Σ current / Σ new = 1.0286, taken over the 30 cells of the six classes above Cursed Bower. That keeps every ratio between cells and makes the six rows sum to the same total as the current matrix, so each delta is a change in a class's relative standing, not in the overall level. Hover a cell for the raw L160 score and its spread.

Score = one player's damage on the boss group ÷ the group's total HP ÷ fight minutes × 100, averaged over the class's Insanity players and the 40 runs. Aura carriers are left out: they don't run Insanity.

Diff

What moved

  • Every class keeps its best boss: Smash Chameleon, Stab Chameleon, Ranged Jellyfish, Fire Badger, Water Badger, Nature DPS Badger.
  • Preferences between two bosses that flip or close up (2). The engine sends each DPS to whichever of the week's two bosses scores higher for their class, so these are the changes that could move players.
  • Smash: Jellyfish vs Hedgehog. The current matrix has Hedgehog ahead by 0.026; the rerun has them tied (0.223 vs 0.223). In a Jellyfish + Hedgehog week, which boss Smash players go to is now a coin flip.
  • Stab: Jellyfish vs Hedgehog. The current matrix has Hedgehog ahead by 0.013; the rerun has them tied (0.291 vs 0.290). In a Jellyfish + Hedgehog week, which boss Stab players go to is now a coin flip.
  • Close calls within a boss. These change which class leads a boss, not where any player goes:
    • Badger, places 1–2: Fire 0.310 now edges Water 0.278 (current: Water 0.301, Fire 0.292).
    • Hedgehog, places 5–6: Water 0.208 now edges Nature DPS 0.205 (current: Nature DPS 0.207, Water 0.197).
    • Swarm, places 3–4: Fire 0.218 now edges Nature DPS 0.212 (current: Nature DPS 0.227, Fire 0.209).
  • Largest moves:
    • Water on Badger −0.023 (0.301 → 0.278)
    • Fire on Badger +0.018 (0.292 → 0.310)
    • Nature DPS on Swarm −0.015 (0.227 → 0.212)
    • Stab on Hedgehog −0.014 (0.304 → 0.290)
    • Smash on Jellyfish +0.014 (0.209 → 0.223)
  • Cursed Bower comes out lower everywhere, by up to 0.038 on Jellyfish, but that row is one player. The other Cursed Bow carries Critical Aura and runs no Insanity, so it isn't scored.

No damage debuff is missing. These bars carry every enemy debuff in the game data that raises damage: Maim, Fracturing Impact, Puncture, Pestilent Shot, Smoke Burst, Frost Surge, Toxic Pollen, the Cursed Bow's curse and the Griffin Bulwark's weaken. The ones they leave out only weaken the enemy: Crippling Slash (−12% enemy damage), Silencing Shot and Stunning Blow.

Limits

What this doesn't settle

  • Small classes are noisy. Stab, Fire and Water have 3 scored players each and Cursed Bower 1. A delta under 0.010 is inside what a different roster or seed would move.
  • Slash isn't rerun. Both Slashes carry an aura (Fierce, Speed), so neither runs Insanity.
  • The current matrix's setup isn't known here. It was read off the Arsenal site on 2 Oct 2026. Normalising removes the difference in overall level, but not any difference in how the two were built.
  • One composition. Debuff uptime and the raid's damage depend on how many of each class are in the raid: 11 Smash, 11 Ranged and 11 Nature DPS here.
