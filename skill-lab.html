MWI Skill Lab

Ability-kit DPS bench · For IC guild Arsenal internal use only. ← Group Battle Trial pages

Build squads of identical players, pick each squad's five ability slots, and run one trial group to completion. Squads sharing the same build are compared against the first of that build, so the workflow for “what if 5 of our 14 crossbows swapped skills?” is: press ＋ variant on that squad, change the copy's kit, and read the vs baseline column. Squads marked support fight normally — their debuffs and Mana Spring's mana feed benefit the whole raid — but their damage is excluded from the analysis, and the five raid auras go to support members first. Mana is real by default (watch the blocked casts column); tick infinite mana to isolate a kit from its mana cost. Players never take damage — this is a damage bench, not a survival test. Slot 0 is the special/aura slot.

Enemy

Trial groups come from the same data the Group Battle page uses. Monster HP and action speed scale with the whole party, support placeholders included.

Options

Auras go to support-squad members when any exist, otherwise to the highest-skill damage dealers, which costs those players a slot. More runs average out roll luck; three is usually enough to separate kits more than 2% apart.

Squads

Each squad is N identical players. ＋ variant moves up to 5 players into a copy of the squad so party size stays fixed. The support checkbox keeps a squad fighting (debuffs and mana feeds still land) while excluding its damage from the analysis — its kit stays editable, e.g. the Water squad's Mana Spring level. A ⚠ on a slot means that ability resolves on a different combat style's accuracy and damage than the squad's weapon — usually a trap.
